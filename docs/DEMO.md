# Bring Vene demonstration

## Local flow (clearly labelled fixtures)

1. Open `/dev`, choose Maria, James or Asha. Ordinary views hide fictional scenario controls; enable the developer checkbox only for synthetic QA. Use separate browser profiles for patient, clinician and family.
2. Prepare two questions, choose what supporting context to share, and approve the question list. Show that guests receive no private source bytes.
3. Join the actual audio room in patient/clinician profiles. Decline processing once: humans can still speak. Agree in both and resume. Demonstrate separate microphone, Vene audio and processing controls.
4. Use fictional input only in the local fixture demo. Show an evidence-linked proposal with uncertain owner or corrected date. Share its source and plan explicitly. A transcript cannot authorize a clinical decision or booking.
5. Request an office window. Show an unanswered priority/private cue and agreed short closeout. Require the responsible clinician's sandbox order and separate patient approval. Change office conditions to lost acknowledgement: status becomes unknown, then an actual persisted receipt confirms the same reservation.
6. In Follow-up upload `fixtures/documents/after-visit-conflict.pdf`. The day/place/medication wording differs. Share with the clinician, review exact quotes and record clarification. Show the plan/reservation were not automatically altered.
7. Choose only title/timeframe/status for a family update, inspect its draft, approve it and show actual sandbox acknowledgement. Create a private next visit; the existing family profile receives 404 for its ID.
8. Restart the owned worker/API under the isolated test controller. Saved follow-through survives, abandoned speech is not replayed, and interrupted processing requires a visible manual resume.

Default fixture interpretation is finite. This demonstration establishes the control/approval/recovery product behavior, not free-form intelligence or recognition quality. Switch to the combined live gate once keys exist and review actual outputs before calling the experience ready to show a founder.

## Unprompted-change review

Invite someone to choose changes without giving them the expected outcome: change the named lab/date, introduce an unclear owner, add an unrelated question, correct supporting evidence, withdraw a family audience while delivery is queued, interrupt closeout, supply a contradictory document, or make the office acknowledge late. Record exact inputs, expected authorization/clinical boundary, observed output/receipt and any wrong assertion. Do not label the deterministic browser suite or a polished recording as an unscripted user test.

The three automated cases provide scenario variation, not a held-out free-form model benchmark. Human patient/clinician acceptance remains separate.
